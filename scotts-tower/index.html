Level 02 Neighbourhoods in the sky
I — The Concept

A city,
stacked.

UNStudio and Ben van Berkel imagined the tower as a series of neighbourhoods in the sky, divided by two sculpted sky frames.

Thirty-one storeys and 231 residences, each cluster with a character of its own. Completed in 2016 for Far East Organization.

II — City View

Orchard,
framed.

City View homes look out over the glow of Orchard Road: the skyline as your daily backdrop, the shopping belt a short stroll below.

III — Park View

Green,
uninterrupted.

Park View homes turn toward the treetops and open sky. Same address, a quieter horizon.
